Privacy policy

This policy explains what the Sangamitra AI Android app and its servers collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. M L Dynamic Solutions Private Limited follows the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India) and the Google Play User Data policy.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, email address and sign-in identifiers (Google account id, Firebase user id) To create your account and sign you in with Google or email and password.
Business profile, workflows and settings you enter To set up and run your AI calling assistant.
Call summaries, caller callback requests and call duration To show the business owner what the assistant handled and to bill call usage.
Audio from your microphone, only while you record a voice instruction in the app To turn your spoken instruction into text and a workflow. The app asks for microphone permission first.
Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) To send you call, payment and service alerts on this device.
Purchase records (plan, amount, Google Play order id) To credit your wallet and keep invoices.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and the app shows no ads. We do not sell your personal data.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging): sign-in, a small user profile record and push notifications.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing; RevenueCat validates each purchase and tells our servers it happened.
  • Sarvam AI: speech-to-text, text-to-speech and language models that run the voice assistant and transcribe your recorded instructions.
  • Razorpay: card and UPI payments in builds not distributed through Google Play.

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. On your device, sign-in tokens are kept in Android encrypted storage.

Sensitive identifiers

Twelve-digit Aadhaar numbers and ten-character PAN numbers are redacted before anything is stored.

Callers

Callers are told that an AI assistant is answering. Call summaries are stored for the business owner who deployed the assistant.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account (see below) we erase your business profile, phone-number binding, tool connections, workflows, vendor documents, callback requests and notification tokens. Payment and invoice ledger entries are kept for at least 6 years because Indian GST law requires it; they are detached from your business name and GSTIN.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and complain about how it is handled. Write to the contact below. You can delete your account yourself, as described at /legal/delete-account.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
