Delete your account

You can delete your Sangamitra AI account and its data in either of two ways.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open Sangamitra AI and sign in.
  2. Tap Voice Agent in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Permanently. Your data is erased and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email info@mldynamic.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete account". We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile record (name, email address and sign-in provider) held in Firebase
  • Your sign-in login. If Firebase asks you to sign in again before it will remove the login, email us and we will remove it for you
  • Your business profile and assigned phone number binding
  • Connected tools, workflows, vendor documents and callback requests
  • Notification tokens

What is kept

Payment and invoice ledger entries are kept for at least 6 years because Indian GST law requires it. They are detached from your business name and GSTIN. Purchase records held by Google Play follow Google's own retention.
