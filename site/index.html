Sangamitra

An assistant that picks up when you can’t.

Sangamitra answers your business phone, takes callback requests and sends you a summary of each call.

Write to us at info@mldynamic.com

What it handles

Calls
Answers your business number and tells callers an assistant is on the line.
Callbacks
Takes a name, a number and a reason, so you can ring back the people who matter.
Summaries
Sends you what each caller wanted. You read a few lines instead of a voicemail.

Why the name

Sangam means confluence, the place where rivers meet. The mark is two streams that keep their own colour as they join into one line, with a third, fainter one running between them.
