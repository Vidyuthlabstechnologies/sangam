## 🎯 PAIN POINTS (Validated via 15 Discovery Calls)

| Pain Point | Current State | Impact | Sangam Solution |
|------------|---------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Vendor Onboarding** | 14-21 days (GST/MCA/CDSCO manual) | Delayed production, audit risk | **2 hours auto-verify** |
| **Vendor Qualification** | Manual audits, paper COAs, no tracking | Failed audits, rejected batches | **Auto-COAs + audit trail** |
| **CDSCO Compliance** | Manual license tracking, state FDA variations | Show-cause notices, import alerts | **Auto-CDSCO + state FDA sync** |
| **NPPA Price Control** | Manual DPCO monitoring, ceiling price | Overpricing penalties | **Auto-NPPA ceiling alerts** |
| **Pharmacovigilance** | Manual ADR reporting, 15-day deadline | Regulatory action, license risk | **Auto-ADR + 15-day timer** |
| **Vendor Payments** | Advance → delivery → manual release | Cash flow issues, vendor disputes | **Escrow + auto-release** |
| **NPPA/Differential Pricing** | Manual ceiling price checks per SKU | Overpricing notices | **Auto-DPCO ceiling per SKU** |
| **Stability/Retention** | Paper records, no alerts | Failed stability studies | **Digital stability + alerts** |
