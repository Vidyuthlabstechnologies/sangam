## 📊 ACCOUNT SCORING MODEL

```
TOTAL SCORE = (Regulatory_Complexity × 2.0) + (Transaction_Volume × 1.5) + (Compliance_Pain × 2.0) 
            + (Payment_Complexity × 1.5) + (Multi_Party_Coord × 1.5) + (Digital_Readiness × 1.0)
            + (Budget_Availability × 1.5) + (Competitive_Intensity × 1.0) + (Switching_Cost × 1.5)
            + (Expansion_Potential × 1.0)
Max Score = 100
```

### Scoring Rubric (1-10 each)

| Criterion | 1-3 (Low) | 4-6 (Med) | 7-9 (High) | 10 (Critical) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| **Regulatory Complexity** | 1-2 govt touchpoints | 3-4 | 5-7 | 8+ (CDSCO + multi-state FDA + NPPA + PV + ICEGATE) |
| **Transaction Volume** | <50 txns/mo | 50-200 | 200-500 | 500+ |
| **Compliance Pain** | Manual but manageable | 10-20 hrs/mo | 20-40 hrs/mo | 40+ hrs/mo, audit failures |
| **Payment Complexity** | Simple advance | Milestone-based | Escrow + milestone | Multi-currency + escrow + milestone |
| **Multi-party Coord** | 1-2 parties | 3-4 | 5-7 | 8+ (procurement/QA/regulatory/finance/warehouse/vendor) |
| **Digital Readiness** | Paper/Excel | Tally/basic ERP | SAP/Oracle + APIs | API-first, webhook-ready |
| **Budget Availability** | < ₹2L/yr | ₹2-5L | ₹5-15L | ₹15L+ |
| **Competitive Intensity** | No incumbent | Tally/basic ERP | Marg/Focus | Veeva/MasterControl |
| **Switching Cost** | Low (no ERP) | Medium (Tally) | High (SAP) | Very High (validated Veeva) |
| **Expansion Potential** | Single site, single product | 2-3 sites | Multi-site, multi-country | Platform play (CDMO + mfg) |
