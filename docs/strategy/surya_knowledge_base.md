# Surya Enterprises: services and documents

Surya Enterprises is a one-stop service centre in Hyderabad for digital, financial and government services.

The document lists below are what people usually bring. They are indicative only. The team always confirms the exact list for each case.

## Aadhaar services
Services: enrolment help, corrections and updates, address and mobile number update guidance, e-Aadhaar download and printing, appointment booking.
Usually bring: proof of identity; proof of address if the address is changing; the mobile phone registered with Aadhaar, if the person has one.

## PAN services
Services: new applications, corrections, reprint and e-PAN download, PAN and Aadhaar linking help.
Usually bring: proof of identity; proof of address; proof of date of birth; a passport size photo; a mobile number.
For PAN and Aadhaar linking: the PAN, the Aadhaar and the registered mobile for verification.

## Banking and financial services
Services: money transfers, cash withdrawals, bank account opening help, balance enquiries and mini statements, credit card bill payments.
Usually bring: bank account details. For account opening: identity proof, address proof and a photo.

## Utility bill payments
Services: electricity, water, gas, mobile and DTH recharge, broadband and landline bills.
Usually bring: the consumer number or a previous bill. For mobile and DTH recharge: the mobile number or customer ID.

## Travel tickets
Services: train, Tatkal, flight and bus bookings, cancellations, booking status, ticket printing.
Usually bring: passenger names and ages, the travel date, and an ID proof for Tatkal.

## EPFO and PF services
Services: PF balance, UAN activation, claim status, PF withdrawal and transfer help.
Usually bring: the UAN, the registered mobile number and bank details. The UAN usually needs to be activated first.

## MeeSeva and government services
Services: income, caste, residence, birth and death certificates, ration card, pension and welfare scheme applications.
Usually bring: Aadhaar, address proof, a passport size photo and supporting papers that depend on the certificate. The team confirms which papers are needed.

## Passport and ID services
Services: passport applications and renewals, appointment booking, voter ID applications and corrections.
Usually bring: proof of identity, address and date of birth, and the old passport for renewal. An appointment may be needed.
Voter ID: proof of age, address and a photo.

## General guidance
Bring original documents and photocopies if possible.
Never share Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, PINs or passwords on a phone call. Nobody from Surya Enterprises will ask for them.

## Not offered at this centre
Driving licence and vehicle services, entrance exam and admission applications, government job applications, printing and photocopy services, and insurance.

## What the team confirms
Fees, opening hours, the address, and how long each service takes. The assistant does not quote these.
